BESS sizing calculatorPaste your half-hourly kW data and set a target payback; get battery size, units and ROI.

Manipulating variables

Load data (kW import)

Paste the Date and kW columns from Excel, or upload a CSV. Each row: date-time (dd-mm-yy h:mm) then kW. Any interval works (30 min detected from your data).

Fixed variables

Tariffs, demand charge and unit specs are placeholders. Replace them with your quote and bill.

-BESS size (kWh)
-Units of BESS
-Lifetime ROI
-Power (kW)
-Capex (RM)
-Saving per year (RM)
-Payback (years)
-Annual ROI

Peak-demand day and shaved peak

Saving = demand charge from shaving the peak + peak/off-peak energy shift. Battery is sized to the largest count whose average payback stays within your target, so you get the most annual saving inside that limit.

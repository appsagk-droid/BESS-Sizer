Selecting a model fills its listed unit price. You can edit the price; manual price or spec edits switch the selector to Custom.

Domestic ToU requires a smart meter. ToU peak: weekdays 14:00-22:00 (about 40 peak hours/week); all other hours are off-peak (about 128 hours/week). Holiday dates use dd-mm-yyyy and are treated as off-peak. Retail charges are shown but excluded from BESS savings because they do not vary with battery dispatch. Monthly maximum demand uses the highest uploaded reading in each month; 30-minute interval data is needed for an exact billed MD. HV energy rates were not included in the supplied schedule, so enter them from your bill.
